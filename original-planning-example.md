# The Lantern Label: an original filled planning example

Original fiction and planning notes by GenToon / Fairrium, October 9, 2026. This is a text plan, not a generated comic or evidence of image quality.

## Source scene and purpose

**Source scene:** At the village repair table, Nora put a blue paper lantern beside a jar of glue. Its label read “NORTH PATH.” Eli arrived carrying a second label: “RIVER PATH.” Nora frowned. The festival map had changed, but their lantern had not. Eli pointed to the new map pinned above the table. Nora peeled off the old label while Eli held the lantern steady. She pressed the new label onto the same spot. “Now we can lead people somewhere dry,” she said. Eli glanced at his muddy boots. “A little late for me.”

**Rights:** newly written fictional scene for this kit; licensed under CC BY 4.0. **Audience/format:** general readers; four panels stacked vertically, dialogue read left to right within each panel. **Beginning:** an outdated route label. **Ending:** the lantern has the corrected label. **Reader takeaway:** a small repair helps others, with a joke about the helper's boots. Omit festival crowds; they are unnecessary to the change.

## Cast and setting

- **Nora:** short curly dark hair, round glasses, green apron over a cream shirt. Wants the route marker ready. Initially does not know the route changed. Starts empty-handed.
- **Eli:** long dark braid, orange vest over a gray shirt, muddy boots. Knows the updated route. Carries the new paper label in his right hand; no other object.
- **Setting:** covered village repair table, late afternoon. Nora is viewer-left, Eli viewer-right. The map is pinned above the table. Blue lantern centered on the table; glue jar viewer-left of it. Keep this arrangement through the scene.

## Dialogue and continuity review

The silent third panel gives the repair room. Read Nora's final line before Eli's response; place their bubbles accordingly. The map need only show the route relevant to this scene. Keep its lettering distinct from dialogue.

**Revision made:** the first draft cropped panel 4 at the waist, hiding the muddy boots. Widening that panel makes the joke understandable through the picture. The plan explicitly assigns Eli's pointing/steadying actions to his left hand so the new label can remain in his right.

**Transition to review:** panel 3 to 4 skips handing over and attaching the replacement label. The old/new labels and discarded strip make that short interval inferable. If a reader cannot follow it, add an attachment panel; do not force the story to remain four panels.

## Episode continuity sheet: filled ending

**Series:** Village Repairs. **Episode:** 1, “The Lantern Label,” plan version 1. **Previous episode:** none. **Elapsed time:** one continuous repair scene. **Accepted plan ending:** corrected lantern remains on the table; Nora and Eli remain beside it. Nora now knows the route changed. Eli's boots remain muddy; no cleaning occurred. Old label lies discarded on the table. Nora and Eli have cooperated on the repair.

**Unresolved thread:** the lantern has not been delivered. **Possible next episode:** carrying it to the river path. That is a proposal, not an established event. Before opening with the lantern outdoors, show or explain its departure from the repair table. Neither character has learned why the route changed in this scene.
